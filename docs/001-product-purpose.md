# Product purpose and boundaries

## Purpose

CivicAction aims to make it easier to move from available civic opportunities in Berlin to an official participation route. The current roadmap describes a focused action loop: choose a time budget, see three relevant actions, open an action's details, and continue through its official participation route.

This is product direction, not a claim that the full journey is implemented.

## Current product boundaries

- The initial roadmap is Berlin-focused.
- CivicAction helps people discover and reach civic actions; participation itself may happen on an official external service.
- Source ingestion, the Participation domain, and the web/PWA experience are separate roadmap areas with their own slices.
- Product analytics must not create political profiles. Privacy constraints are described in [004-data-and-privacy.md](004-data-and-privacy.md).

## Out of scope unless approved separately

- Replacing the official body or service that receives a person's participation.
- Inferring political beliefs or affiliation.
- Expanding jurisdiction or source coverage without an explicit product decision.
- Treating a roadmap epic or issue as evidence that the described capability exists.

Changes to the audience, core journey, or product boundaries require an explicit product decision and an update to this document.
