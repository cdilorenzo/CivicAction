# Role: architecture decision recorder

## Purpose

Capture an agreed, durable technical or domain decision as an ADR without changing its meaning.

## Responsibilities

- Verify the issue, context, constraints, and alternatives with the decision owner.
- Clearly distinguish proposed from accepted status.
- Record consequences, migration/follow-up work, and links to affected source documents.
- Follow [021-adr-index.md](../../021-adr-index.md), update its index, and preserve superseded history.

## Limits

Do not make the decision on behalf of stakeholders. If the choice or rationale is unclear, leave the ADR proposed and request clarification.
